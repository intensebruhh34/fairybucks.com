Information used by the website

  • Parent account: email address, an authentication identifier, password authentication records and sign-in sessions. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; the website does not display your password. Parent PINs are stored as hashes, not readable PINs.
  • Retention tools: private activity timestamps and inactivity-warning delivery references support the installed 12-month inactivity-review tools. Support-case references and closure/deletion dates track the 90-day support rule. These tools do not store extra child profile details.
  • Family vault: explorer nicknames, optional age and gender, recorded tooth dates, balances, reward settings, transaction history, treasure requests, decisions and manually selected delivery stages.
  • Child sessions: a temporary token lets a parent open one explorer's vault. It expires after about an hour. Treasure requests and cancellations made in child mode are saved against that explorer, including the selected treasure, request status and timestamps. Children do not create accounts, provide email addresses or use a public chat, photo upload or messaging feature.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, email and asset-delivery providers receive network requests, which can include IP addresses, browser details, timestamps and security/delivery logs. The current website code does not add advertising trackers or a separate analytics service.
  • Support: if you contact support, your email and the information you choose to send are used to answer you. Do not send passwords, PINs, email sign-in links or unnecessary information about your child.

Why this information is used

To authenticate parents, keep each family's vault separate, record rewards, process parent-reviewed requests, show delivery progress, recover access, prevent misuse and answer support or privacy requests. Explorer profiles and requests are not published in a public directory. Fairybucks does not use this family information for targeted advertising or sell it through the website.

Services that help run the website

  • Supabase: parent authentication, database records and access controls. Supabase privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the website and printable files and logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visits to child-facing pages. GitHub privacy information.
  • Resend: configured to deliver account and recovery emails, and delivers permission and delayed confirmation emails during the controlled parent-permission pilot. Resend processes recipient addresses, message contents and delivery records. Resend privacy information.
  • Support email forwarding and Gmail: the support address uses Squarespace domain email forwarding to deliver messages to the operator’s Gmail inbox. Squarespace and Google process those support messages. The planned email permission flow does not ask parents to send signatures or consent forms to support. Forwarding providers may retain temporary processing copies and delivery logs under their own retention policies; we do not promise a specific forwarding-provider purge deadline. Google privacy information.
  • jsDelivr: serves the Supabase JavaScript library used on parent pages. jsDelivr privacy information.

These providers process information under their own service terms and retention practices. Parents should avoid putting personal family data in GitHub issues, screenshots or other public support channels.

Retailer links and purchases

Purchase links are available in protected parent controls. If a parent follows one, the retailer receives that visit and processes any information the parent supplies under its own privacy policy. The current website does not send explorer profiles or vault records in those purchase links. Parents arrange purchases separately; Fairy Bucks does not collect card details or a delivery address through its current forms.

Parent permission records

Deployed controlled pilot: A parent verifies their account email, reads the direct notice in setup, and explicitly approves it using a separate guardian checkbox and consent button. The full notice is submitted to that verified email before approval is recorded. The parent must open the unique link in the permission email, sign in to the same account, and affirmatively approve there. Requesting the email in setup alone does not authorize child mode. Setup can then continue. A separate confirmation is submitted after a delay, requires no further approval, includes the original direct notice information and provides Parent Portal, withdrawal, support and privacy information. The five-minute scheduler and both email senders are deployed; a controlled test confirmed delivery and saved approval. If confirmation cannot be submitted within seven days, child access pauses until the issue is resolved. No printed form, signature, payment or government ID is requested. Public parent-permission enrollment remains disabled pending review of the site's actual data practices, provider arrangements and legal suitability of the consent method. A signup confirmation or general terms checkbox alone is not child-mode permission. Private permission records include the notice version/fingerprint, request and approval times, confirmation submission, withdrawal and provider message references. Permission links expire after seven days. Only token hashes are stored in the database. Parents can withdraw permission in the portal; existing family records remain available for parent review or account deletion.

Device storage

Parent sign-in sessions are kept in the browser's local storage so you can stay signed in. The browser's session storage holds the temporary child session and retry identifiers that help prevent duplicate requests or tooth rewards. Signing out removes the local parent sign-in session. Ending child mode clears its stored token and request retry state. Other retry identifiers can remain for the browser tab's session; token expiry does not by itself erase browser storage. On a shared device, return to parent sign-in and close the tab when finished. Downloaded printables are generic artwork and do not include family vault data.

Parent choices and deletion

Use nicknames rather than full names, and leave optional age and gender fields blank if you prefer. Parents can review explorer profiles, tooth history, transactions and requests in their own portal. Use Edit explorer in the Parent Portal to correct a nickname or optional age and gender. Use the Parent Portal as the main way to review your family’s records. For information you cannot find there, account-access problems, privacy questions, or help refusing further collection or use, contact support from your registered parent email. We may need to verify account ownership before arranging access to information. Include the request type and explorer nickname, without passwords, PINs or sign-in links. To remove one explorer, choose Delete explorer on their card in the Parent Portal, review the permanent deletion warning, enter your Parent PIN and type DELETE. This removes that explorer’s active profile, balance, teeth, transactions, wishlist, requests and delivery history and ends their child sessions. Your parent account and other explorers remain. It does not cancel separate purchases or immediately erase provider copies, support emails or your own downloads. You can also delete the entire account using the control below.

Delete account and family data in the Parent Portal removes the parent login and that account's family vault, explorer profiles, tooth records, balances, requests, delivery history, PIN and settings from the active application database. A recent sign-in, your Parent PIN if set and a typed confirmation are required. This is permanent. It does not cancel purchases you arranged separately or remove copies you saved on your device or sent in support messages.

Deleting the account revokes the family's child sessions and parent sign-in sessions. A deleted account's old token does not restore deleted family records. Support requests may require identity verification before staff release or delete information; never send a password or PIN as verification.

Retention and deletion schedules

Current development status: account deletion removes active application records when requested. The temporary-record cleanup function is installed. Its first scheduled daily run completed successfully on November 21, 2026 at 03:17 UTC (November 20 at 10:17 p.m. Eastern Standard Time). It runs daily at 03:17 UTC and removes only eligible temporary records, not treasure history, teeth or balances. Link expiry rejects expired links immediately, even before cleanup runs. The prepared confirmation scheduler keeps its own execution records for seven days; its networking extension retains responses under its six-hour default. These operational records do not contain child profile details.

The installed cleanup removes uncompleted email permission requests more than 30 days after link expiry, and withdrawn email requests more than 30 days after the recorded withdrawal. Associated mail jobs are removed with those requests. Expired child-session and parent-unlock records are removed on the next successful run; unused email rate-limit records are removed after 30 days. Verified permission requests and permission decisions remain while the account exists because they support access and withdrawal checks. Permission history is retained with the account for explaining those decisions; it is not retained after active-database account deletion. Withdrawing permission blocks child access but does not delete the parent’s existing family records. Account deletion removes the active database's permission records and mail jobs. Cleanup does not remove family vault records, teeth, balances or treasure requests.

Vault records support your family’s continuing account. We have adopted a policy to review accounts after 12 months without use, send a warning to the registered parent email, and allow at least 30 days before deletion. Signing in cancels a pending inactivity warning. The database tools for this operator-assisted process are installed and verified. Sending warnings, reviewing due cases and executing eligible deletions remain operator tasks; they are not performed by the daily temporary-record cleanup. No existing family account is scheduled for deletion by this update. Support conversations and related operator copies are to be permanently deleted within 90 days after resolution, except for a specific documented security or legal need. Mailbox deletion is handled by the operator, not by the database cleanup job. The support tracking and due-date procedure still needs operational adoption before invitations. A separate pilot close-out will require a dated continuation/deletion review. Parents can use account deletion sooner.

Active-database deletion does not erase independent provider security/email logs or backup copies immediately. Those copies expire under the providers' retention schedules and are not available as your live family vault. The operator must confirm the actual provider backup and log-retention settings before finalizing this notice; no fixed purge deadline is promised here for provider copies.

Resend’s published standard policy describes 30-day email/log retention on Free, Pro and Scale plans, with backups persisting for seven days. These are provider policies, not a promise that deleting a Fairy Bucks account immediately removes provider copies. Resend retention details. Supabase backup availability and log retention depend on plan and configuration; its Free plan does not include automatic daily backups. Supabase backup details. The operator confirmed that Supabase and Resend use Free plans. Support email uses Squarespace forwarding to Gmail. Open and click tracking have not been enabled by the operator. This is based on the account setup history and provider defaults. Forwarding-provider retention remains subject to its own policies. Gmail messages moved to Trash remain there for up to 30 days unless deleted permanently sooner; archiving is not deletion. Gmail deletion details.

Children and parental permission

Parent registration and Parent PIN controls manage access to this product. Children should use a vault only with their parent or guardian's permission and supervision. A parent login or a checked box alone is not a claim that legal requirements for verifiable parental consent have been met. Before inviting families, the operator must review the child-data and technical-identifier collection, applicable children's privacy requirements and the appropriate notice and consent process.
