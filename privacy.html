Information used by the website

  • Parent account: email address, an authentication identifier, password authentication records and sign-in sessions. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; the website does not display your password. Parent PINs are stored as hashes, not readable PINs.
  • Family vault: explorer nicknames, optional age and gender, recorded tooth dates, balances, reward settings, transaction history, treasure requests, decisions and manually selected delivery stages.
  • Child sessions: a temporary token lets a parent open one explorer's vault. It expires after about an hour. Children do not create accounts, provide email addresses or use a public chat, photo upload or messaging feature.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, email and asset-delivery providers receive network requests, which can include IP addresses, browser details, timestamps and security/delivery logs. The current website code does not add advertising trackers or a separate analytics service.
  • Support: if you contact support, your email and the information you choose to send are used to answer you. Do not send passwords, PINs, email sign-in links or unnecessary information about your child.

Why this information is used

To authenticate parents, keep each family's vault separate, record rewards, process parent-reviewed requests, show delivery progress, recover access, prevent misuse and answer support or privacy requests. Explorer profiles and requests are not published in a public directory. Fairybucks does not use this family information for targeted advertising or sell it through the website.

Services that help run the website

  • Supabase: parent authentication, database records and access controls. Supabase privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the website and printable files. GitHub privacy information.
  • Resend: configured to deliver account and recovery emails. Resend privacy information.
  • jsDelivr: serves the Supabase JavaScript library used on parent pages. jsDelivr privacy information.

These providers process information under their own service terms and retention practices. Parents should avoid putting personal family data in GitHub issues, screenshots or other public support channels.

Parent permission records

The pilot uses a returned signed parent consent form and operator review before new explorer profiles or child-mode access are enabled. A verification request or checkbox alone does not enable access. The private database records the notice version and fingerprint, request/verification/withdrawal dates and an internal reference to the reviewed form. Signed forms are handled through the support inbox and must be kept in restricted operator storage, not public repositories. Parents can withdraw child-mode permission in the portal; existing family records remain available for parent review or account deletion.

Device storage

Parent sign-in sessions are kept in the browser's local storage so you can stay signed in. The browser's session storage holds the temporary child session and retry identifiers that help prevent duplicate requests or tooth rewards. Signing out or ending a child session clears the relevant session state. Downloaded printables are generic artwork and do not include family vault data.

Parent choices and deletion

Use nicknames rather than full names, and leave optional age and gender fields blank if you prefer. Parents can review explorer profiles, tooth history, transactions and requests in their own portal. To correct profile details, request a copy of family information, ask privacy questions or stop further collection, contact support from the registered parent email.

Delete account and family data in the Parent Portal removes the parent login and that account's family vault, explorer profiles, tooth records, balances, requests, delivery history, PIN and settings from the active application database. A recent sign-in, your Parent PIN if set and a typed confirmation are required. This is permanent. It does not cancel purchases you arranged separately or remove copies you saved on your device or sent in support messages.

Deleting the account revokes the family's child sessions and parent sign-in sessions. A deleted account's old token does not restore deleted family records. Support requests may require identity verification before staff release or delete information; never send a password or PIN as verification.

Retention

Vault records are kept to provide your family's account while it is active. During the invite-only pilot, the operator will review unused accounts and remove pilot family records within 90 days after the pilot ends, unless a parent chooses to continue with the service. Parents can use account deletion sooner. Signed consent forms are retained while the permission is active and reviewed for deletion within 30 days after withdrawal or account deletion, subject to a specific documented legal need. Other support correspondence is reviewed for removal within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless a longer period is needed to resolve an outstanding security or legal matter.

Active-database deletion does not erase independent provider security/email logs or backup copies immediately. Those copies expire under the providers' retention schedules and are not available as your live family vault. The operator must confirm the actual provider backup and log-retention settings before finalizing this notice; no fixed purge deadline is promised here for provider copies.

Children and parental permission

Parent registration and Parent PIN controls manage access to this product. Children should use a vault only with their parent or guardian's permission and supervision. A parent login or a checked box alone is not a claim that legal requirements for verifiable parental consent have been met. Before inviting families, the operator must review the child-data and technical-identifier collection, applicable children's privacy requirements and the appropriate notice and consent process.

Changes

We will update this page when the site's data practices change. Material changes involving children's information may require further notice and permission before they take effect.
