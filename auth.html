Print Fairy Bucks

Choose just the denomination you need, or download the mixed starter sheet. Each PDF has four notes on one letter-size page.

Printing from a phone? Choose Open PDF to print below. This opens the sheet without forcing a download. In Safari on iPhone, tap the three-line menu next to the website address, then Share → Print.

    Phone printing help

    iPhone / iPad (Safari): Open the PDF first. On iPhone's compact toolbar, tap the three-line Page Menu just to the left of the website address, then Share → Print. If your toolbar already shows the Share button (a square with an upward arrow), tap that instead, then Print. You may need to scroll down through the Share actions to find Print. Use an AirPrint-compatible printer on the same Wi-Fi network.

    Android: Open the PDF, then look for Print in the PDF viewer's menu. In Chrome, use More (three dots), Share, then Print if available. Your phone needs a compatible printer or print service.

    No Print option? If you opened Fairybucks inside another app, open it in Safari or Chrome. You can also use Download PDF, open the saved file in Files or a PDF viewer, and print from there. Your printer's app may offer another option.

    Phone print settings vary. Select letter-size paper and Actual size / 100% when offered. Try one page first; if the printer adds scaling you cannot change, use the saved PDF from another viewer or print from a computer.

    Print single-sided at 100% / Actual size on letter-size paper. Cut along the dashed guides outside the notes. These sheets contain fronts only.

    Printing Fairy Bucks does not change your child's vault balance. Record the tooth reward separately in Parent controls. The paper represents that recorded reward; it is not an extra reward.
