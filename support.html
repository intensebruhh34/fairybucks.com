Parent Help

Parent Portal

For grown-ups: get help with your family vault, account access or a privacy request.

Contact support

support@fairybucks.com

Before family invitations: the operator needs to configure and test a monitored support inbox. No response-time guarantee is offered yet.

Write from your registered parent email when possible. Include the page you were using, the action you tried and the error message. For a screenshot, hide private family information first. Never send passwords, PINs or sign-in/reset links.

Parent permission

The parent-permission workflow is staged and is not active during development testing. Once activated for the family launch, open Parent permission in the portal to read the notice and request verification. Then print the consent form, sign it and return a readable scan/photo from your registered parent email to support. Do not include a child’s photo/full name, government ID, password or PIN. Child mode stays paused until the operator reviews your form. You can withdraw permission in the portal; this ends child sessions and keeps existing records available for parent review or deletion.

Account access

Use Reset password on the sign-in screen. For a forgotten Parent PIN, sign in and choose Reset Parent PIN, then open the email sign-in link. A PIN reset does not change the account password.

Delete account and family data

Sign in to the Parent Portal and select Delete account and family data. The review screen explains what is removed. Deletion requires a recent sign-in, your Parent PIN if set, and typing DELETE. All explorers and vault history belonging to that account are permanently removed, and the parent login is deleted.

If you cannot sign in, contact support to request deletion. Staff must verify account ownership first. Deleting a Fairybucks account does not cancel a separately arranged purchase.

Review, correct or request information

Parents can review their own family records in the portal and undo mistaken tooth entries in Parent controls. Contact support to correct explorer details, request a copy of information or discuss stopping further collection. Please use a nickname when describing a child in your message.
