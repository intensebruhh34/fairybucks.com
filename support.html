Parent permission

The parent-permission workflow is staged and is not active during development testing. The planned flow sends the direct notice to your confirmed parent email. Open the secure link, sign in to the same account, read the notice and explicitly give permission if you choose. Opening a link or confirming signup alone does not give permission. A separate confirmation email explains withdrawal. No printed form, signature, payment or government ID is requested. Once the reviewed flow is activated, child access and new explorer profiles require verified permission. Parents can withdraw permission in the portal to end child sessions while keeping existing records available for parent review or account deletion.

Account access

Use Reset password on the sign-in screen. To set your first Parent PIN, sign in and choose Create Parent PIN in the portal; no explorer is needed. For a forgotten Parent PIN, choose Reset Parent PIN beside its entry field, then open the email sign-in link. A PIN reset does not change the account password.

Delete account and family data

Sign in to the Parent Portal and select Delete account and family data. The review screen explains what is removed. Deletion requires a recent sign-in, your Parent PIN if set, and typing DELETE. All explorers and vault history belonging to that account are permanently removed, and the parent login is deleted.

If you cannot sign in, contact support to request deletion. Staff must verify account ownership first. Deleting a Fairybucks account does not cancel a separately arranged purchase.

Delete one explorer

In the Parent Portal, choose Delete explorer on that explorer’s card. Review the named explorer and the permanent deletion warning, enter your six-digit Parent PIN and type DELETE. If you have not created a PIN, set one up first. Deletion removes that explorer’s active profile, balance, teeth, transactions, wishlist, requests and delivery history and ends their child sessions. Your parent login and other explorers remain. This cannot be undone and does not cancel separate purchases. Provider logs, backups, support messages and your own copies are handled separately as explained in Privacy. Contact support if you cannot sign in; ownership must be verified before staff act.

Request a copy of family information

Contact support from your registered parent email to request a copy. This is handled after verifying ownership; there is no self-service download button. Never send passwords, PINs or sign-in links.

Review, correct or request information

Parents can review their own family records in the portal and undo mistaken tooth entries in Parent controls. Use Edit explorer in your explorer list to correct a nickname or optional age and gender. Contact support to request a copy of information or discuss stopping further collection. Please use a nickname when describing a child in your message.
